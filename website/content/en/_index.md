On 2 May 2017, a repository called **weloveinterns** appeared on GitHub. Its first commits were "Initial commit", "Initial Home page" and "Created _Sidebar" — back then it was still a wiki, holding interns' materials, assignments and reports, like a noticeboard by a lab door.

Nine years on, the same repository holds 779 commits, 500 of them from one person (lazyparser, Wu Wei). Its signboard has changed four times: Zhongke Chongde, PLCT Lab, Kubuds, Project Jiachen; in the Bingwu year of 2026 it gained another layer of name, the **Five-Colour Stone Project**. Only 10 files remain today; 286 have existed over its history — on 12 October 2024 a single commit deleted 253 of them, gathering up every intern report from 2021 and earlier, and left only a forwarding address in the README: `git checkout 664048d`.

But the repository's name has never changed: **we love interns**.

Only two things on this line have been genuinely stable: a joint programme that opens every year, and batch after batch of interns who come for three months, three years, or just long enough to submit a single PR. Most come and then leave, and are now written into the MAINTAINERS files of other companies, other foundations, other open-source projects — which is exactly the result this system was meant to produce.

This site wants to do one thing: lay out those names and that line for you to see.
