## Under what licence this site's content is published

**CC BY-SA 4.0** (Attribution — ShareAlike 4.0 International).

One detail is worth spelling out: people often say in conversation that "the repository is CC BY", but the repository's `LICENSE` file is actually **Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International** — that is, CC BY-SA 4.0, with the extra ShareAlike condition. The text of this site is largely rewritten and excerpted from that repository, so this site is also published under **CC BY-SA 4.0** (not the more permissive CC BY 4.0), to stay consistent. If you reuse text from this site, credit "weloveinterns.org" and keep the same licence.{{< cite "repo" >}}

## Where the material comes from and how it is handled

- **All diagrams are drawn here**: the "support-system loop" on the home page and the Five-Colour Stone page is an SVG drawn for this site, with its structure following page 44 of the CCF talk; **no screenshots, no reproduced images**;
- **No third-party images and no web fonts**: the favicon is a hand-drawn SVG, and the type uses the system font stack;
- **No full reproductions**: WeChat articles, talk scripts and papers are summarised and briefly quoted, with a link to the original;
- **Data quoted ≤ the conclusion**: for papers and surveys only the statistical conclusions in their public abstracts are cited (with definitions and dates), and the scope of those conclusions is stated — for example, the authors of the Stanford paper themselves describe their findings as "descriptive indicators, not causal estimates";
- **How the talks are handled**: the two talk PDFs were provided to this site by their author, and are used only to restate the speaker's own views;
- **Names and endorsements**: intern entries include only material made public in the repository with the person's agreement or by their own PR; endorsement numbers link directly to Project Jiachen's public repository.
