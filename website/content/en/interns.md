## First, the rules of this list

This page is taken from the repository's `outstanding-interns.md`. It includes only students whose **effective internship lasted more than 3 months** and who **agreed to appear or submitted the PR themselves**; those who did not agree are not written in, so many interns who have since graduated and started work, but missed this page when it was created, are not on the list.{{< cite "repo" >}}

There are only two categories: **LV4+ (Great Ability / Super Ability)** and **senior interns**. The "endorsement" link after each entry points to Project Jiachen's mutual-recognition talent network; every endorsement is publicly verifiable on GitHub.{{< cite "builders-network" >}}

## In their own words

The first two entries on the list (the robotics period, 2018–2019) keep the interns' own text, which is better than any summary:

> During my graduate studies, the internship opened a door to another world for me. Internship life was wonderful, and I gained a wealth of practical experience in robot software development… I also grew and matured quickly, completing the transition from student to employee. I am grateful for that time.
> A word to the interns who come after: students, reading ten thousand books is not enough — you also have to walk ten thousand miles. Come and intern; the scenery here is different.
> — An Chuanxu (LV5+)

> Besides learning some professional knowledge, during the internship I also picked up skills that have been enormously helpful in later work — using git, team collaboration processes and conventions, coding conventions, how to look things up quickly, and so on. I went from being a novice to making a lot of progress in a short time… Some of my fellow interns are still good friends today.
> A word to the interns who come after: the clumsy bird flies first, the rookie goes home late; study hard, and ask when you don't understand.
> — Wang Xiaoyun (LV4)
